# Legacy telematic prior art / 旧遥现场景

Read these resources only after the author explicitly selects an architecture comparable to the Co-here case: multiple venues, local performer phones and local sound engines, inter-venue control relay. They document one former workflow, not general PNDS conversion policy.

- [Co-here case](case-co-here.md): concrete source evidence and unresolved questions.
- [Triple inventory](input-validation.md) and [analysis](analysis.md): format-specific inspection where the materials actually apply.
- [Legacy port map](port-map-template.md): selected telematic-case fields; omit or extend fields based on the author-confirmed plan.
- [Conversion notes](conversion-playbook.md): mechanical p5/SC/TouchOSC adaptation after platform and artistic decisions.
- `design/adr/` records historical reasons for the old architecture; it does not override the current author-confirmed plan or installed App contract.

The previous mandatory triple, automatic source-plus-bundle delivery, sound-first fidelity ranking, fixed Internal/hub/role/seat model, “never synchronize continuous controls” and unconditional drift tolerance are retired as general rules. Each may describe a historical case only. Current workflow begins with arbitrary-source assessment and author confirmation. The current packaging procedure is [packaging](packaging.md).
